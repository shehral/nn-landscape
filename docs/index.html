Los Alamos lab white-box method aggregates multi-token logit KL-divergence for VLM hallucination detection, +9.4% accuracy and +14.8% AUROC over single-probe baselines.

Product
MTRE is not document-specific, but the multi-token aggregation principle applies to per-field extraction confidence in OCR-3 output. If token-level reliability signals are accessible at inference time, they could flag phantom-row candidates without a secondary verification call.
