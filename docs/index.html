Mirage Probes: How Vision Models Fake Visual Understanding (arXiv 2606.13870)

Mirage Probes (arXiv 2606.13870): VLMs generate confident, coherent answers to image-grounded questions even when the image is absent or uninformative.

Product
The mirage hallucination class — confident outputs decoupled from visual input — is a failure mode relevant to OCR pipelines: a model might produce plausible-looking structured JSON from a low-quality or blank scan without signaling uncertainty. The behavioral signature (high confidence, low visual grounding) differs from phantom-row hallucinations and warrants a separate evaluation protocol.
Research
The probe-based detection approach in 2606.13870 is complementary to the team's activation-patching and logit-lens work; it operates on intermediate representations without requiring ground-truth document annotations. Worth reading alongside the earlier Role-Break paper (arXiv 2607.29412) to compare detection signal quality.
