Patterns this build

  • Docling (IBM Research) and MinerU are consolidating as the default open-source parsing backends for self-hosted document AI stacks; both haiku.rag and localOllamaRAG integrate one of these rather than building extraction from scratch, indicating that the parsing-layer decision is effectively made for many developers by open-source community defaults.
  • High-throughput inference acceleration for open-weight OCR models (TurboOCR: TensorRT FP16, >200 img/s) is emerging as a distinct market segment targeting production on-premise deployments, where latency and cost dominate accuracy considerations.
  • Community benchmark notebooks and demo repos consistently group Nanonets, DeepSeek-OCR, and HunyuanOCR into the same evaluation cluster, but the comparison baseline appears to be Nanonets-OCR2-3B (open-weight) rather than OCR-3 (API-only), meaning the publicly visible competitive picture is two model generations stale.
  • Enterprise agentic document pipelines increasingly pair a parsing layer (LiteParse, Docling, MinerU) with vector-plus-graph storage (LanceDB, OpenSearch, Neo4j), suggesting that differentiation among document-AI vendors is moving toward retrieval and reasoning capabilities rather than raw extraction accuracy.
