Patterns this build

  • Attention heads are emerging as the primary mechanistic unit for VLM hallucination localization: two independent papers this build (2608.07302 and 2608.24966) converge on head-level analysis rather than layer-level or neuron-level approaches.
  • General-purpose vision benchmarks (Roboflow Vision Evals) and document-specific benchmarks (IDP Leaderboard, OmniDocBench) are now measuring different capability profiles for the same models; Claude Fable 5.1 ranks #2 on OCR in Roboflow but has no confirmed score on document-centric benchmarks.
