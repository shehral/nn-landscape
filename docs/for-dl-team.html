OCR-EDR: Rendering-Aware Diagnosis and Repair for Closed-Loop OCR Improvement

Closed-loop system renders predicted OCR output back to image, diagnoses mismatches against the source, and applies targeted repairs before final response.

Product
The rendering-simulation diagnosis step is applicable post-extraction: simulating what OCR-3 predicted would look like visually and comparing to the source document can surface layout and structural errors before the caller receives output. Implementation cost is bounded by rendering fidelity.
Research
The closed-loop repair signal is a complementary error source to logit-lens and activation-patching probes; rendering-based error detection may surface structural hallucinations that attention-based probes miss.
