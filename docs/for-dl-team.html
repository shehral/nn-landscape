  • The document-parsing market is converging on 'single model replaces multi-tool stack' as the primary competitive positioning frame. Reducto r-1, Mistral Agentic Search, and MinerU2.5 all invoke this framing; OCR-3's 'only OCR model you need' positioning predates the current wave. Differentiation will depend on verifiable benchmark claims, not the positioning frame.
  • VLM hallucination mitigation is splitting into two methodological camps across this build's items: training-time (INFUSE: bidirectional contrastive objectives; HunyuanOCR-1.5: GRPO RL) and inference-time/training-free (Region-Aware Attention Recalibration; Role-Break attention head analysis). Training-time methods report larger reduction rates but require fine-tuning access; inference-time methods are deployable against existing checkpoints.
  • OmniDocBench score comparisons have become unreliable for competitive positioning: OCR-3's launch score of 90.5 (version unspecified) now sits below MiniMax M3 (91.6% on v1.5) and MinerU2.5-Pro (95.69% on v1.6), but v1.5 and v1.6 scores are not directly comparable due to the April 10 benchmark update. The benchmark version ambiguity is a recurring gap across multiple items this build.
  • Efficiency-scale inversion is appearing in OCR: PP-OCRv6 at 34.5M claims to beat Qwen3-VL-235B on recognition tasks; MinerU2.5 at 1.2B approaches OCR-3-scale OmniDocBench performance. Both are open-source. This pattern — small specialist models closing the gap to VLM-scale generalists — is consistent across multiple document-AI architectures this build.

INFUSE: Hallucination mitigation via implicit feature stabilization, 46–63% reduction, no inference overhead (arXiv:2608.29924)

INFUSE stabilizes VLM representations via bidirectional contrastive objectives, reducing hallucinations 46–63% on Qwen3-VL-8B with zero inference overhead

Product
No direct product implication. INFUSE is a training-time modification requiring access to fine-tuning infrastructure, not a deployable inference-time workaround.
Research
INFUSE targets feature instability as the root cause of hallucinations, stabilizing representations around perturbation-averaged anchors with bidirectional contrastive objectives. The method has no inference overhead. On Qwen3-VL-8B — a decoder-family model with architectural lineage overlap with OCR-3 — it achieves 46–63% reduction. An ablation on OCR2-3B or OCR2-1.5B-exp would validate the method on a document-OCR architecture and is the closest available published comparison point to the team's phantom-row mitigation work.
