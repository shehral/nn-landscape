Both require no model modification and are applicable to closed-weight production models — a convergence toward runtime mitigation over training-time interventions.

  • MinerU2.5 (Shanghai AI Lab) and HunyuanOCR-1.5 (Tencent) both published CVPR/ACL accepted papers with open-source MIT-licensed models this cycle. Chinese AI lab open-source OCR releases continue to narrow the gap to commercial API accuracy at zero licensing cost, lowering the barrier for developer-tier adoption of alternatives to OCR-3.
