No single benchmark covers all four, meaning high scores on any one may not translate to the others.
  • Agentic document extraction platforms — LandingAI ADE Gen2 (Sep 9), Reducto r-1 (Sep 1), Nutrient Data Extraction API (Sep 10) — all launched in the first two weeks of September 2026, each emphasizing single-pass or agentic workflows, per-character or flat-rate pricing, and grounded citations. The pricing-and-architecture convergence suggests a commodity floor is forming in the API-document-extraction market.
  • EMNLP 2026's DocInsights workshop and Dr.DocBench challenge formalize difficulty-aware document parsing evaluation: pages are curated by inter-parser disagreement, filtering out the easy majority that all strong systems already solve. This methodology — sampling from the model disagreement frontier rather than the whole benchmark distribution — is an emerging evaluation standard distinct from population-level metrics.

Real5-OmniDocBench: A Full-Scale Physical Reconstruction Benchmark for Robust Document Parsing in the Wild

Real5-OmniDocBench tests 1,355 OmniDocBench pages across 5 physical capture conditions to measure OCR robustness under real-world acquisition.

Product
Document parsing products that score well on standard benchmarks may degrade significantly on physically-acquired documents (warped, photographed under poor illumination, skewed). Real5-OmniDocBench provides the first controlled attribution of degradation by capture condition, which is directly relevant for any OCR product marketed to customers who process scanned or mobile-captured documents.
Research
The benchmark's factor-wise degradation attribution — digital vs. each of five physical conditions, with identical GT annotations — would allow the team to quantify OCR-3 or OCR2-3B performance drops by capture condition rather than as a single aggregate score.
