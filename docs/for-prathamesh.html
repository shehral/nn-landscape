Patterns this build

  • Three frontier labs shipped multimodal document-capable models within 72 hours (Fable 5.1 Sept 1, Gemini 3.8 Flash Sept 2, GPT-6 Astra Sept 3-4), all at $10/M input or below, all with PDF or image input. The cadence of frontier-lab document capability releases has materially accelerated.
  • Document extraction APIs are differentiating on citation fidelity alongside raw accuracy: Nutrient leads with 'source-grounded' extraction claims, Reducto r-1 emphasizes per-element provenance, and MinerU2.5 exposes reading-order and bounding-box metadata. Citation granularity is emerging as a second commercial axis.
  • Open-weight document parsers are approaching closed-API accuracy: MinerU2.5-Pro (95.69 on OmniDocBench v1.6) and HunyuanOCR-1.5 with GRPO-trained hallucination mitigation both represent self-hostable models within benchmarking distance of API products, lowering the switching cost for mid-tier deployments.
  • VLM hallucination research is converging on two complementary technical tracks visible in this build: feature-alignment stabilization (INFUSE, arXiv 2608.29924) and circuit-level functional decomposition (Transcoders, arXiv 2605.22902). The two approaches target the same failure mode at different levels of the stack and are additive rather than competing.
  • xAI's announced-but-unshipped model cadence is now a structural feature of the competitive landscape: Grok 4.7 has missed three deadlines and is being superseded in announcement by Grok 4.8 before 4.7 ships. This differs from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI's release patterns and warrants a standing policy of scoring xAI models only on confirmed publication of a model card.
