Causally identifies OCR attention heads in VLMs (incl. Qwen3-VL-8B) as general semantic heads; verbalization lens shows language-aligned image representations from layer 0.

Product
OCR heads govern both text-reading and non-textual semantics in the same head subset; this implies structural hallucinations and phantom rows may arise from the same circuit, not a text-specific failure mode. This reframes where targeted interventions on OCR-3 should look.

Retrieval Heads Meet Vision: Uncovering How VLMs Locate and Extract Visual Information

Visual Retrieval Heads (VRHs, ~1.7-2.6% of heads) causally govern text-to-region grounding across 11 VLMs; masking top 20 drops grounding accuracy by up to 80 points.

Product
A small, causally necessary head subset controls all visual grounding in tested VLMs. Disrupting these heads is a candidate mechanism for phantom-row generation in OCR-3, where a table row appears ungrounded in the source image.
Research
The unified head-scoring framework spans 11 VLMs and 5 referring-expression benchmarks. Running the VRH identification protocol on OCR2-3B alongside the OCR-heads method from arXiv 2609.18823 would produce a two-piece circuit map (what-heads vs. where-heads) for the team's hallucination interpretability work.
