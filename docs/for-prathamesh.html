  • Three top-tier frontier models (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Gemini 3.8 Flash) shipped within a four-day window September 1-4 with image+PDF input but no published IDP Leaderboard or OmniDocBench scores.   • GRPO reinforcement learning is converging as the post-SFT alignment method for document OCR VLMs: HunyuanOCR-1.5 uses verl-based GRPO, 'Seeing is Believing?' proposes a multi-objective GRPO reward for OCR hallucination, and FireRed-OCR-2B (prior builds) used Format-Constrained GRPO.   • Mechanistic circuit attribution for VLM hallucinations is an active research direction across at least two papers this build: arXiv 2606.08777 (counterfactual circuits) and arXiv 2602.11824 (sparse latent steering), both complementary to the team's existing interpretability stack. The research front is moving toward inference-time interventions rather than training-time fixes.
