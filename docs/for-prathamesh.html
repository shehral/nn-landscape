Patterns this build

  • Risk calibration and abstention are emerging as first-class OCR-VLM capabilities: three papers this build independently approach VLM OCR from the angle of selective confidence and abstention rather than accuracy maximization, signaling a research convergence around reliability engineering for production OCR pipelines.
  • Table understanding is becoming a multi-layer evaluation problem: papers on DELTA, Visual-TableQA, and READoc all decompose table accuracy into physical structure, logical structure, and semantic content — a shift from TEDS string-match to structure-aware metrics that tolerate fewer phantom-row and cell-misassignment failures silently.
  • Competitive VLM technical reports are arriving months after initial model releases, providing delayed but authoritative architectural detail for top-ranked IDP Leaderboard models: Qwen3-VL (Nov 2025 paper), HunyuanOCR (Nov 2025), and Qianfan-VL (Sep 2025) all released technical reports well after their benchmark appearances.
  • Multilingual document benchmarking infrastructure is maturing independently of the IDP Leaderboard: MDPBench and the DELTA Hindi benchmark (TORQUE) both introduce structured multilingual evaluation surfaces covering document types and scripts systematically absent from OmniDocBench.
  • Hallucination mitigation is diversifying across three parallel tracks — training-time preference optimization, inference-time latent probing, and risk-calibrated generation — with no single approach yet dominating; all three are applicable to OCR-specific failure modes rather than general VLM hallucination only.
