Three distinct actors converging on cost-adjusted quality as the leading argument.

Extend's RealDoc-Bench (this build) follows Reducto's LongExtractBench (July 2026) and Extend's own prior OCR benchmarks. Each vendor selects document types where its system performs best; without third-party auditors, the signal is limited.
  • VLM hallucination taxonomies are fragmenting into fine-grained categories across consecutive builds. This build adds semantic substitution and orthographic normalization (arXiv 2607.24077) to the taxonomy; prior builds added TTH, Implicit Feature Stabilization, VISOR, and INFUSE. The field is converging on multi-category frameworks rather than single aggregate metrics.
  • Training-free hallucination interventions are emerging as a distinct subfield. Spectral QK Weight Steering (this build), Implicit Feature Stabilization (arXiv 2608.29924, prior build), and TTH (arXiv 2608.11474, prior build) all propose no-retraining corrections at different levels: weight-space, feature-space, and token-validation. Three complementary mechanisms in two consecutive builds.
