RIKER inverts document Q&A evaluation: generates documents from known ground truth, enabling annotation-free hallucination measurement at 172B-token scale across temperature and context-length conditions.

Product

Research
RIKER's paradigm inversion — generate document from truth, not extract truth from document — avoids annotation cost and benchmark contamination simultaneously. 22 arxiv

Revealing Multi-View Hallucination in LVLMs: MVH-Bench + RSCD decoding, up to 94.8-pt improvement (arXiv 2603.23934, rev. Sep 1 2026)

MVH-Bench (4,800 QA pairs) characterizes cross-instance and cross-view hallucination in LVLMs; RSCD training-free decoding yields up to 94.8-pt improvement on Qwen2.5-VL.

Product
Cross-instance hallucination — model errors caused by visual similarity between adjacent regions — maps directly to phantom-row hallucination in tables where similar rows cause copy or duplication errors. RSCD's attention-masking approach could be tested on OCR-2's architecture as a lightweight inference-time mitigation.
Research
MVH-Bench's taxonomy (cross-instance vs cross-view hallucination) adds precision to the team's hallucination classification framework. RSCD is training-free and requires only attention-mask surgery at decoding time, lowering the reproduction barrier. The Qwen2.5-VL results provide a direct reference since OCR-2 series share decoder-family lineage.
