Patterns this build

  • Frontier labs (Anthropic, Google, DeepSeek) shipped three multimodal models in an eight-day window (Fable 5.1 Sept 1, Gemini 3.8 Flash Sept 2, DeepSeek V4.1-Flash Sept 10), none with published IDP Leaderboard or ParseBench document-benchmark scores. The evaluation lag means customers comparing these models against OCR-3 must wait for independent benchmark runs rather than relying on vendor data.
  • Open-weight VLMs under 1.5B parameters now lead the current OmniDocBench v1.6 leaderboard: NaviDC-OCR (1.2B, 96.87) and MinerU2.5-Pro (1.2B, 95.69) both exceed OCR-3's published 90.5, which was measured before the April 10, 2026 benchmark update. Compact architecture plus deformation-aware and two-stage decoupled designs are closing the gap with large-scale models.
  • Three independent mechanistic interpretability papers (INFUSE, Transcoders, Circuit probing) converge on a testable hypothesis: VLM hallucinations trace to specific, localizable mechanistic features — feature instability under perturbation, transcoder-decomposed MLP sublayers, and causal circuit effects. The convergence across three methodologies strengthens the case for an interpretability-first approach to hallucination mitigation.
  • Single-model collapse of multi-component parsing stacks is now the dominant architectural and commercial narrative: Reducto r-1 (commercial, $0.01/page) and the Cost-Quality VLMs paper (academic, 35B MoE on single H100) both frame their contributions as replacing multi-tool pipelines with one model. The convergence across commercial and research tracks signals that multi-stage pipelines are being treated as a solved problem worth simplifying.
  • Benchmark versioning fragmentation continues: NaviDC-OCR, MinerU2.5-Pro, Gemini 3.8 Flash, and Claude Fable 5.1 each publish scores on different evaluation frameworks (OmniDocBench v1.6, Roboflow Vision Evals, Roboflow Data Extraction) with no shared reference point. OCR-3's published 90.5 has an unspecified OmniDocBench version. Without a versioned re-evaluation, customer-facing comparisons involving OCR-3 and any of these models cannot be made without a version qualifier.
