  • Sub-2B-param models now lead OmniDocBench v1.6: OvisOCR2 (0.9B, 96.58), NaviDC-OCR (1.2B, 96.87), and MinerU2.5-Pro (1.2B, 95.69) all score above OCR-3's published 90.5. Nutrient's GA this week adds a third new entrant with an existing enterprise distribution channel.
  • Circuit-analysis approaches to VLM hallucinations are converging across three simultaneous papers (Transcoders arXiv 2605.22902, Dual-Pathway Circuits 2605.13156, Circuit Probing 2606.08777), all using sparse computational graphs or causal patching to localize and predict hallucination behavior. A shared methodological framework is consolidating.
  • Multi-step retrieval is emerging as a distinct layer above extraction: Mistral Agentic Search's FinanceBench improvement from 26.7% to 86% via five document operations illustrates that the most-cited accuracy gap in enterprise document workflows is retrieval architecture, not OCR accuracy — a different competitive layer than endpoint-based extraction.
